# iNaturalistGG Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

iNaturalistGG is a browser extension that adds game-style stats to iNaturalist. This policy covers what it does with your data.

## What the extension uses

- **Your iNaturalist username**, which you type into the extension.
- **Your public iNaturalist data:** observations, identifications and leaderboard positions. This is the same data anyone can see on iNaturalist.org.

## Where it goes

- The extension sends requests only to **iNaturalist's public API** (`api.inaturalist.org`) and its image hosts (`static.inaturalist.org`, `inaturalist-open-data.s3.amazonaws.com`). Those requests include your username or user ID, so iNaturalist can return your data.
- Everything the extension calculates (lifers, ranks, badges, pioneers) is stored **only in your browser**, in the extension's own storage.
- The developer runs **no servers** and receives **none** of your data. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. Your data on iNaturalist itself is unaffected; iNaturalistGG only reads it.
